Talqeen

The reciter reads a line, then goes silent for exactly as long as that line took, so you recite it back — then continues. This is how every halaqa on earth teaches. It works with the screen off.

The Veil

The text is removed from the real page a step at a time: full, faded, the first word of each line, blank. The geometry never moves, because where a line sits on the page is part of what a hafiz has memorised. Press and hold to peek.

The Fork Drill

A phrase appears twice in the Quran and your mouth continues into the wrong surah. The audio plays up to the exact word where the two part, cuts, and waits for you — then plays the correct continuation, and immediately the other one, so you hear both futures of the same past. 1,335 such phrases, generated from the text rather than trusted.

What it will not do

No streaks, points or badges — the documented failure mode in hifz is measuring your pace against someone else's, so it shows pages owed and never a flame. No AI marking your recitation. No account, no sync, no prayer times, no qibla. It is a mushaf.
