Privacy Policy

Al-Mau'iza (الموعظة) — last updated 26 August 2026

The short version. This app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. What it saves — your place in a recitation, your favourites, your settings, and any audio you download — is stored on your own device and stays there. The only requests it makes are for the recitation audio itself.

What is stored, and where

All of the following is held locally by your browser, in IndexedDB and local storage, and never transmitted:

  • The reciter, surah and position you last listened to.
  • Your favourites, chosen language, theme and display preferences.
  • Recitations you choose to download for offline listening, and audio files you import yourself from your device.
  • Memorisation practice records — which pages you have added, when you last reviewed them, and where you tapped to mark a stumble. The app does not listen to or assess your recitation; it has no way to, and does not try.

Deleting a download in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes all of it.

Where the audio comes from

Playing or downloading a recitation fetches audio from the archive that publishes it. Most of it comes straight from archive.org, and some from download.quranicaudio.com or server8.mp3quran.net.

A few reciters' audio sits behind links that have to be looked up or that expire, and those requests pass through mushaf-audio.mushaftarteel.workers.dev, a proxy operated by the developer of this app.

Like any web request, these carry your device's IP address to whichever host serves the file. They carry nothing else about you: there is no account, no identifier, and no cookie. No request is made to any host other than those listed above — the app ships a Content-Security-Policy that permits exactly these and blocks everything else.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or fingerprinting.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts, sign-in, email collection or contact list access.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions are requested.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anybody, because nothing is collected.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It is not directed at children specifically and contains no social features, user-to-user contact, or user-generated content.

Changes

If a future version of the app collects or transmits anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
